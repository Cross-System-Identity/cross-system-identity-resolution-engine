⚡ Enterprise Platform Capabilities & Core Architecture
🔒
Privacy-Preserving Record Linkage (PPRL)

Utilizes salted HMAC-SHA256 tokenization and Bloom filter similarity analysis to perform high-precision record linkage across databases without exposing raw identifiers.

🔑
Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP) Verification

Integrates non-interactive Fiat-Shamir ZKP cryptographic protocols, allowing citizens to prove ownership of their credentials without transmitting secret keys.

📊
Adaptive Scoring & Resolution Engine

Automatically adjusts resolution matrix between intra-system (40-20-15-15-10) and cross-system (50-40-5-5) modes based on source and target identity types.

📜
Immutable Regulatory Audit Logging

Records all match executions, verification requests, and compliance events into a centralized audit log for regulatory reporting and real-time inspection.

🌐
Multi-System Credential Federation

Federates identity records seamlessly across healthcare (ABHA), identity (Aadhaar), document vaults (DigiLocker), tax (PAN), voter, mobile, and email systems.

🛡️
Strict PII Masking & Data Protection

Enforces privacy compliance by automatically masking raw values (e.g., Aadhaar, Phone numbers) as [PROTECTED] in all client-facing responses and logs.
